About This Volume

The Building Has Been Leaning on You.

"Beverly Loraine Greene spent her professional life calculating structural load paths. She understood, at an engineering level, the difference between a structure designed to distribute its load and a structure that concentrates strain on members never meant to carry it alone. She calculated load paths for a living. She also happened to work in a body that the institution had designated as one."

This volume is the response to the Loud Noise of November 2024 — the sound of millions of load-bearing members, specifically Black, Indigenous, and Women of Color, simultaneously realizing that the building they were holding up was never designed for their safety. Volume 3 is not about resilience. It is about resistance. Not the ability to absorb damage — but the refusal to be the system's shock absorber.

The Practice: U.N.M.A.S.K. Framework

Six Pillars of Personal & Institutional Transformation

"Recalibration is the transformation journey. U.N.M.A.S.K. is the method we use to walk it."

Each pillar represents a stage in the practice of unmasking, moving you from the exhaustion of survival armor to the verticality of authentic authority.

U
Unlearn
Break agreement with what the institution has taught you about your own limits and worth.
N
Navigate
Move through hostile environments with strategic intentionality rather than reactive survival.

Govern your energy, authority, and boundaries from a position of structural clarity.

Release the performance, the weight, and the apology that were never yours to carry.
K

Stand in the full authority of what you know about yourself and the systems around you.
The Process: Recalibration Framework

The Macro Theoretical Model

Recalibration is the systems-level methodology for identifying, dismantling, and redesigning oppressive structures. It is the "What" that governs our work.

Diagnose what is misaligned. Deconstruct what is false. Recalibrate to what is true. Rebuild what is just.

Phase 01 · Diagnose
See Clearly
Name what is actually happening across Mind, Systems, Community, and Body.
  • What is misaligned?
  • Who benefits from this condition?
  • What has been misnamed?
→ Clarity
Phase 02 · Deconstruct
Disrupt the Lie
Break agreement with distortion and interrupt normalized patterns.
  • What is false?
  • What must be unlearned?
  • What am I no longer agreeing with?
→ Disruption
Phase 03 · Recalibrate
Return to Truth
Realign mind, body, and authority with justice and dignity.
  • What is true?
  • What aligns with justice?
  • What does God reveal?
→ Alignment
Phase 04 · Rebuild
Create What Should Exist
Design new ways of living and leading that reflect structural integrity.
  • What needs to be built?
  • What does justice look like?
  • How do I live differently now?
→ Transformation
